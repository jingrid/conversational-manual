Front protective cover
Turn the cover (bayonet fitting) in the direction of
the arrow and remove.
NOTE: The front protective cover may only be removed
when the camera is cocked (fully wound).
Rear protective cover
Depress the catch and swing back the cover. Lift
the cover off the magazine support hooks.
Opening the focusing hood
Lift the lid firmly at the rear edge, and swing it up
to the vertical position.
The built-in magnifier
The magnifier flips up into the viewing position
when the oval button is moved in the direction of
the arrow.
To re-fold the magnifier simply press it down
with a fingertip until it locks back into the lid.
The magnifier may be changed for a correction
lens. See page 13.
Closing the focusing hood
"Pinch" in the side plates at the hinge points,
and fold down the lid.
