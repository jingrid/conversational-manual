## Front protective cover

Turn the cover (bayonet fitting) in the direction of the arrow and remove.

**NOTE:** The front protective cover may only be removed when the camera is cocked (fully wound).

[Photo: Side view of the camera with the focusing hood lid swung up toward the vertical position; a red curved arrow shows the upward swinging motion of the lid.]

[Photo: Rear view of the camera body with the rear protective cover fitted over the back; a red arrow on top points down onto the catch, indicating it should be depressed.]

## Rear protective cover

Depress the catch and swing back the cover. Lift the cover off the magazine support hooks.

## Opening the focusing hood

Lift the lid firmly at the rear edge, and swing it up to the vertical position.

## The built-in magnifier

The magnifier flips up into the viewing position when the oval button is moved in the direction of the arrow.

To re-fold the magnifier simply press it down with a fingertip until it locks back into the lid.

The magnifier may be changed for a correction lens. See page 13.

## Closing the focusing hood

"Pinch" in the side plates at the hinge points, and fold down the lid.
