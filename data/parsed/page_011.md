# Film magazines

## Attaching the magazine

Ensure that the magazine slide is fully inserted, and that the indicator is white. If the status indicator does not show a white signal then refer to the instruction on page 9.

Locate the magazine on the lower support hooks – see that it is fully seated on the supports.

Slide the magazine catch to the right and hold it there whilst swinging the magazine forward into contact with the camera's rear plate. Release the magazine catch, ensuring that it returns to the locked position on the left side.

Remove the magazine slide and the camera is ready to shoot.

[Photo: Rear corner of the camera with a film magazine attached. Two red arrows point to the magazine catch on the side of the magazine, and a curved red arrow at the upper rear shows the magazine being swung forward into contact with the camera body. No callout labels or numbers.]

## Removing the magazine

Ensure that the camera is cocked (fully wound on).

The magazine and camera indicator windows should both display white. (If the magazine indicator window shows a red signal then follow the instructions on the next page.)

Insert the magazine slide into the magazine.

Slide the magazine catch to the right.

Swing back the magazine, and lift it off the lower supports.

The magazine cannot be removed without inserting the magazine slide. The slide protects the film from fogging. Note also that the camera cannot be fired when a magazine, with slide inserted, is attached to the camera.

[Photo: Film magazine (left) separated from the camera body (right), showing the open rear of the camera. A red line at the top connects the indicator windows on the magazine and the camera, and a red curved arrow at the bottom shows the magazine's path off the lower supports. The number "12" is visible on the side of the magazine. No callout labels.]
